How to Install StreameX on Android TV & Firestick

StreameX includes native TV remote navigation (D-Pad), Netflix-style cards, and 1080p/4K full-screen playback. Follow either beginner-friendly method below:

Get Downloader App

On your Android TV, Google TV, or Firestick, open the Google Play Store (or Amazon Appstore) and install the free app:
"Downloader by AFTVnews".

2

Allow Unknown Apps

Go to TV Settings > Device Preferences / My Fire TV > Developer Options > Install Unknown Apps. Toggle Downloader to ON.

3

Enter Download Link

Open Downloader, select the URL box on the home tab, and type:
streamex.pages.dev/download
or your direct APK link, then press Go.

4

Install & Enjoy

The APK will automatically download. When the pop-up appears, click INSTALL. StreameX is now added to your TV Apps row!
